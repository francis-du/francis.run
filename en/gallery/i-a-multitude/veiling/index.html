Silence Reveals Me

Before you see this mountain, it rests with you in stillness. When you come to see it, the mountain becomes clear in an instant. Nothing lies outside this heart-mind. Colour is without colour; form is without form. Within the small chamber of the mind, suchness appears.

Francis Du · Mountain guide
